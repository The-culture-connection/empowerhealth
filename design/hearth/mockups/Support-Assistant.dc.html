What does the iron supplement do, and when should I take it?

Iron helps your body make the extra blood you and your baby need, and it can ease the tiredness that comes with low iron. Many people take it once a day with water or a little orange juice. Your midwife can tell you the dose and timing that fit you best, so it is a good question to bring to your next visit.
