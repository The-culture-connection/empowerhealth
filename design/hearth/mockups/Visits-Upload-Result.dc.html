Important Next Steps

  • Start your iron supplement. Take it once a day, the way your midwife showed you.
  • Repeat blood work on November 4 to check that your iron is coming up.

  • Behavioral health intake call. Someone from the office will call you to set up a first conversation.

Medications Mentioned

Iron supplement: Helps bring your iron level back up. Take it once a day with food.

Key Medical Terms Explained

Anemia: When your blood has fewer healthy red blood cells than it needs. It can make you feel tired.

Anatomy ultrasound: A detailed scan, usually around 18 to 22 weeks, that looks at how your baby is growing.
